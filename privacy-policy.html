Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Gems Mark ("we," "us," or "our") operates this website, where we sell handcrafted 925 sterling silver rings and pendants. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using our site, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you give us:

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Shipping and billing address
  • Order details (products purchased, ring size, customisation notes)
  • Payment information — we do not store your full bank or Payoneer account details; these are exchanged directly for the purpose of completing manual payment
  • Messages you send us through contact forms, WhatsApp, or email

Information collected automatically:

  • IP address, browser type, device type
  • Pages visited, time spent on site, referring website
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 6)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Process and fulfil your orders, including shipping and customer service
  • Communicate with you about your order, inquiries, or custom requests
  • Improve our site, products, and customer experience
  • Send promotional emails or updates, only if you have opted in
  • Prevent fraud and maintain the security of our site
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Shipping and courier partners (FedEx, SkyNet Worldwide Express, UPS, DHL) to deliver your order
  • Service providers who help us run the site (e.g., website hosting, database, email tools)
  • Legal authorities, if required to comply with the law or protect our rights

If you also purchase from us via Etsy or eBay, please note that those platforms have their own separate privacy policies governing that transaction.

4. Payment Information

Payments on our site are made via Bank Transfer or Payoneer. We do not process card payments directly through this website — payment account details are shared with you after checkout, and you complete payment manually with your own bank or Payoneer account.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, including order history for accounting and legal purposes, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

6. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep items in your cart, understand how visitors use our site, and improve site performance. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though some features of the site may not work properly as a result.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your information
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Object to or restrict certain processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 10.

Note: if Gems Mark sells to customers in the EU/UK or California, this section should reference GDPR or CCPA specifically — we'd recommend confirming the exact wording with a legal professional before relying on it.

8. Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
